Open My Heart 💌

Wifey, welcome to September. 🌷

Another month has arrived, and honestly, I don't want to simply say "Happy September" and leave it at that. I want you to know what is inside my heart as we enter another chapter together.

We've already experienced so many things together. We've had beautiful days, difficult days, moments where we understood each other perfectly, and moments where we didn't know what to say.

We've made mistakes. We've learned from them. We've struggled with our emotions, our thoughts, our fears, and sometimes even with ourselves.

But Wifey, I don't want our mistakes to become the entire story of us.

I want September to be another chance.

Another chance to communicate better. Another chance to understand each other. Another chance to forgive. Another chance to grow. Another chance to choose what is right.

And most importantly, another chance to keep growing closer to the Lord.

I know sometimes you overthink whether you're getting closer to God or not. I know there are moments when you feel guilty because you don't pray as much as you used to, or because you make mistakes even when you genuinely want to do better.

But I hope you understand something, Wifey: struggling doesn't automatically mean you're moving away from Him.

Sometimes growing means realizing how much you need Him. Sometimes growing means falling, recognizing it, and choosing to get back up.

I don't want to be someone who pulls you away from God. I want to become someone who encourages you to seek Him more, even when I have to grow and change myself.

I love you deeply. But I also want to learn how to love you properly—with patience, honesty, self-control, forgiveness, faithfulness, and understanding.

I don't want our relationship to only be about happy moments. I want it to teach us how to become better people.

So if September becomes difficult, please don't think you have to carry everything alone.

When you're tired, rest. When you're confused, pray. When you're scared, breathe. When you make a mistake, learn. When you feel far from God, come back.

And when you need someone to remind you that you're loved, I'll be here.

I'm not asking you to be perfect, Wifey. I don't want perfection from you because I know I'm not perfect either.

I simply want us to keep trying.

One day at a time. One prayer at a time. One lesson at a time. One choice at a time.

Happy September, Wifey. 🌷

Whatever this month brings, I hope you remember that you are deeply loved, appreciated, and worth fighting for in the right way.

Always here,
Hubby 💞
